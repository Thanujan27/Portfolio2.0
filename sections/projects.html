Hertshire – AI-Powered Recruitment and Candidate Ranking Platform ↗

Developed an AI-assisted recruitment platform designed to evaluate and rank candidates against job requirements using natural language processing. The platform compares traditional TF-IDF matching with semantic similarity using Sentence Transformers and incorporates supplementary technical evidence from GitHub repositories, including technology usage, code-related evidence, development activity, and contribution metrics. Implemented a candidate-ranking workflow with evidence-based scoring, ranking comparisons, ablation studies, and sensitivity analysis to investigate how different matching approaches and evidence weightings influence candidate rankings.
